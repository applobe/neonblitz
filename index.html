NEON BLITZ

Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 25, 2026  ·  Last updated: November 25, 2026

This policy explains what Neon Blitz does with information on your device. In short: your progress is stored locally, you do not need an account, and the only outside service is Google's ad SDK for optional rewarded ads.

Android app com.applobe.neonblitz No account required Ads only when online

01

At a glance

  • No sign-in: we do not ask for your name, email address, phone number, or contacts.
  • Progress stays local: scores, achievements, weekly levels, and settings are saved in private app storage on your device.
  • Ads are optional: the “Watch Ad to Continue” option is hidden when you are offline, and no ad is loaded.
  • One third party: Google Mobile Ads (AdMob) processes ad-related data under Google's Privacy Policy.

This page is a plain-language summary of the app's behavior and is intended to complement, not replace, the data safety declarations in the Google Play listing.

02

Scope

This policy applies to the Neon Blitz Android application, package com.applobe.neonblitz, published by APPLOBE. It does not cover third-party services reached through ads or the share sheet, or your device's own advertising settings. Those services have their own privacy policies.

03

Information stored on your device

Neon Blitz uses Android's private SharedPreferences storage. The information below never leaves your device through the app.

DataExamplesWhy it is storedSent by the app?
Settings Sound, music, vibration, screen shake, graphics quality, frame-rate choice To remember your preferences between runs No
Scores Highest score and top run scores To show your best results and history No
Achievements Unlocked tier, points toward the next tier, current earn multiplier To track progression and unlock visual skins No
Weekly levels Completion status and timestamps for Crown, Ace, and Conqueror To show weekly goal progress No
Shared image cache A generated progress image created when you tap “Share Progress” To hand the image to the Android share sheet Only if you choose a recipient app

You can remove scores, achievements, and weekly progress with Settings → Reset progress. Your settings are kept. Clearing the app's data or uninstalling the app removes everything stored locally.

04

Data handled by ads

Neon Blitz includes the Google Mobile Ads SDK (AdMob) to show an optional rewarded ad that restores shields after game over. When you are online, that SDK may collect and share the following categories of data, as described in Google's disclosures:

CategoryExamplesPurposesShared?
Device or other IDs Advertising ID, app set ID, and identifiers for signed-in accounts where applicable Advertising, analytics, fraud prevention Yes
Approximate location General location estimated from an IP address Advertising, analytics, fraud prevention Yes
App interactions App launches, taps, ad interactions, and video views Advertising, analytics, fraud prevention Yes
Diagnostics Launch time, hang rate, energy usage, and performance signals Advertising, analytics, fraud prevention Yes

This data is processed by Google's ad SDK under Google's Privacy Policy and Google's advertising technologies policy. We do not use the advertising ID ourselves. Data handled by the SDK is encrypted in transit.

If you are offline, the rewarded-ad option is hidden and no ad request is made. You can also simply choose not to watch the ad.

05

Why the data is used

Advertising or marketing

To display the rewarded ad, support ad delivery, and measure how ads perform.

Analytics

To understand ad delivery, app performance, and diagnostic signals such as hangs or energy use.

Fraud prevention, security, and compliance

To detect invalid traffic, protect the ad ecosystem, and enforce platform policies.

The advertising ID in this app is not used for app functionality, developer communications, personalization, or account management. The game has no accounts and no personalized content feed.

06

Who information is shared with

  • Google LLC (AdMob): receives ad-related data described above to deliver and measure ads.
  • Advertising partners: may receive ad-related data through Google's ad ecosystem, as described in Google's policies.
  • The app you choose in the share sheet: receives the progress image and text only when you tap “Share Progress” and pick an app. We do not collect information about who you share with.

We do not sell personal information for money, and we do not include separate analytics, crash-reporting, or social-login SDKs in Neon Blitz.

08

Retention and deletion

  • On-device data: kept until you reset progress, clear the app's data, or uninstall the app.
  • Ad data: retained and handled by Google under Google's Privacy Policy.
  • Advertising ID: can be reset or limited at any time in your Android device's Google Ads settings.
09

Your choices

  • Play offline, or skip the rewarded ad; the game is fully playable without watching ads.
  • Reset or delete your advertising ID in Android Settings → Google → Ads. Menu names may vary by device.
  • Limit ad personalization in your Google account's ad settings.
  • Use Settings → Reset progress to clear scores, achievements, and weekly levels.
  • Clear app data or uninstall Neon Blitz to remove all locally stored information.
  • Contact us to ask about data or request deletion assistance.
10

Security

Progress and settings are stored in Android's private app storage, and ad-related data handled by the ad SDK is encrypted in transit. No system can be guaranteed completely secure, so please keep your device locked and up to date.

11

Children's privacy

Neon Blitz is not directed to children under 13, or under 16 in regions where a higher age applies. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the app, please contact us so we can address it.

12

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the app or its SDKs change. The “Last updated” date at the top of this page will change, and material changes will be reflected in the app's Google Play data safety declaration.
